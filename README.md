# Shopping-Mall-Sales-Analysis

**Project Overview**:
  This project analyzes sales data from 10 shopping malls to understand customer purchasing behaviour, revenue patterns, and payment preferences. The result is an interactive Excel dashboard built for a board meeting business case.

**Business Objectives**:
Examine sales performance across malls and product categories.
Identify customer purchasing patterns.
Compare revenue across age groups and gender.
Understand which payment methods customers use most.

**Dataset**:
Records: 99,457 customers
Malls: 10
Key fields: Gender, age, quantity, price, payment method, shopping mall.

**Tools Used**:
Microsoft Excel 
Pivot Tables
Pivot Charts
Slicers

**Data Cleaning**:
Checked for missing values.
Identified and removed duplicate records.
Removed empty rows.
Grouped customer ages into four categories: Young Adult, Adult, Senior, and Elderly (42 and above).

**Dashboard Features**:
Summary cards for total customers, total revenue, and total quantity.
Customer count and revenue split by gender.
Revenue share by age category.
Payment method usage, overall and by age category.
Customer traffic by shopping mall.
Category slicer to filter the full dashboard.

**Key Findings**:
The dataset holds 89,370 customers. 
Total revenue is £68.55M. Females generated £40.93M (60%) and males generated £27.62M (40%).
Customers aged 42 and above (Elderly) generated 54% of revenue. Young Adult, Adult, and Senior each contributed 15% to 16%.
Cash is the most used payment method with 44,447 transactions. Credit card follows with 34,931 and debit card with 20,079.
Cash leads in every age category. Elderly customers pay by cash most often with 23,794 transactions.
Mall of Istanbul and Kanyon draw the most customers, each near 20,000. Several malls sit below 5,000.

**Recommendations**:
Grow male participation. Run targeted promotions in the categories men buy most.
Keep Elderly customers loyal. Offer loyalty rewards and premium options, and add offers to attract younger age groups.
Keep cash handling efficient in every mall. Test small incentives to encourage card payments.
Lift lower-performing malls. Study what draws traffic to Mall of Istanbul and Kanyon, such as events, store mix, and location, then apply it elsewhere.
